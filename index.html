01About

An engineer who likes to build.

I'm studying Electrical and Computer Engineering at the USC Viterbi School of Engineering, where I'm on the avionics team at the Rocket Propulsion Laboratory building flight electronics for liquid-propellant launch vehicles. Before that I spent four years in Science Olympiad, where I designed and iteratively tested turbine geometry in CAD and took second at the state level.

The other half of my work is software. I designed and published Terra, a native Android app that turns on-device location history into an animated, exportable video, and I shipped Room Check, a multiplayer game on Roblox with its own economy and client state synchronisation. What connects all of it is the same thing: I like taking something from a rough idea to a finished object that other people can actually use.

02Projects

Things I've shipped.

  • 2026 · Android · Google Play

    Terra

    A native Android travel-history tracker. It logs coordinate and timestamp data on-device, then reconstructs it into an animated playback of everywhere you've been — with a rendering pipeline that composites the route into an exportable MP4. Built in Kotlin with Jetpack Compose and published to the Google Play Store.

    • Kotlin
    • Jetpack Compose
    • Android SDK
    • On-device rendering

  • 2025 — 2026 · Roblox

    Room Check

    A round-based multiplayer building game. Every player gets a room plot and a build budget, decorates it from a 113-item catalogue with grid-snapped placement, then the whole server tours each room and rates it one to five stars. I wrote the server-authoritative round loop, the placement system, DataStore persistence and the in-game economy in Lua.

    • Lua
    • Roblox Studio
    • Multiplayer
    • MCP
